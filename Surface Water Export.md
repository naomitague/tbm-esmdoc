

Some key ideas

*  concentration-discharge relationships vary from place to place, time to time
* a substantial amount of N-export occurs during high-flow extreme events, and these can access legacy nitrogen
* total N-export depends both on concentration and discharge (so mean concentration can be declining but total export can still increase)
