# Table S5 — Catchment-scale studies of forest change impact on evapotranspiration

Source: Yuting et al., 2023 (Supplementary Table S5)

ΔF: change in forest coverage (%). ΔET: change in evapotranspiration (%).
Method codes: PWE = paired catchment experiment; QPW = quasi-paired catchment; HM = hydrological modelling; EA = elasticity analysis; SH = combined statistical methods and hydrographs.

Continent, Köppen-Geiger climate class, and Whittaker biome are assigned from each catchment's listed lat/lon using standard Köppen-Geiger world maps and published site descriptions for well-known experimental catchments (e.g. Coweeta, HJ Andrews, Fernow, Fool Creek/Fraser). These are best-estimate classifications, not pulled from a gridded GIS dataset, so treat borderline zone edges (e.g. Cfa/Dfa, BSk/Csa) as approximate.

**Flagged coordinate issues (not corrected below, just noted):**
- **#101 Mabegondo**: listed as 8.3, -43.2 (mid-Atlantic Ocean). Mabegondo is a real research station in Galicia, Spain (~43.25 N, -8.22 W) — looks like a transposed lat/lon in the source. Classified as Spain; verify against Yuting et al.
- **#69–70 HF1 / HFW1** (Boggs et al., 2016): listed as 36.2, 78.9, which falls in the arid Kunlun/Tibetan-Plateau-margin region of western China — implausible for a forested catchment study. Classified using the listed coordinates but flagged as likely erroneous; verify against source.
