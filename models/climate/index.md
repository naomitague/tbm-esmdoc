# Climate Model Overview

*In development.*

This section will document the climate side of the framework — starting with
the meteorological forcing that drives the other models: precipitation,
temperature, radiation, humidity, wind, and atmospheric deposition (the
"Inputs" row at the top of the water and vegetation diagrams) — and,
later, land–atmosphere coupling and feedbacks.
